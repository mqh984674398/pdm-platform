## 2. 请您决定 / Decisions for you

**Q1 正常老化如何分级 How should normal aging be graded?** 健康参考保持冻结（契约要求），所以老化会持续增大偏差。目前规则只看偏差大小，老化 100 天后会到预警甚至严重。Golden Case 期望正常老化最多为“关注”。
The healthy reference stays frozen (as the contract requires), so aging keeps growing the deviation. Today the rules look only at its size, so after about 100 days aging reaches WARNING and even CRITICAL. The Golden Cases expect normal aging to stay at WATCH at most.

- **推荐 Recommended:** 批准 G3，并在 T024（证据融合）中加入老化分级：缓慢、稳定、符合部件寿命的漂移最高为“关注”；安全规则、突变或加速漂移照常升级。 Approve G3 and add aging grading in T024 (evidence fusion): a slow, steady drift consistent with the component's age stays at WATCH at most; safety rules, abrupt changes and accelerating drift escalate as now.
- 保持现状：老化超过阈值就升级到预警或严重。 Keep as is: aging past the thresholds escalates to WARNING or CRITICAL.
- 暂不批准，先修改（请说明要改什么）。 Hold G3 for changes (say what to change).

其余行为（下列 B1–B5）随 G3 一并批准；如需修改，请指出编号。 The behaviors below (B1–B5) are approved with G3; to change one, name its number.

- **B1 何时显示“未知” When health is UNKNOWN:** 无特征数据、当前安装没有健康参考、维护后验证进行中、最新可评估窗口比本机最新数据晚 24 小时以上。 No features, no verified reference for the current installation, post-maintenance validation running, or the newest evaluable window is more than 24 h behind the machine's newest data.
- **B2 告警保留 Alarms persist:** 告警只能由人关闭；规则回落后告警标为“已回落”但仍待处理；更换部件后旧告警也需人工关闭。 Only people resolve alarms; when the rule stops firing the alarm shows "cleared" but stays open; after a replacement the old alarms still need resolving.
- **B3 维护后自动推进 Post-maintenance workflow:** 稳定 4 h、验证 36 h（数据不足可延长到 120 h）；改善或无变化自动回到正常监测，部分改善、恶化或仍无法判断进入需复核，复核只能由工程师关闭。 Stabilization 4 h, validation 36 h (extended up to 120 h without comparable data); IMPROVED or NO_CHANGE returns to normal monitoring automatically, PARTIAL, DETERIORATED or still UNKNOWN goes to review, and only an engineer closes a review.
- **B4 迟滞 Hysteresis:** 等级立即上升，连续 3 个窗口低于阈值才下降；持续缓慢偏移由 CUSUM 提示“关注”。 Levels rise at once and drop after 3 windows below the threshold; a sustained small shift raises WATCH through the CUSUM.
- **B5 正式报警 Official alerting:** 仍未启用。告警只是复核记录，不发送通知；启用正式报警或连接生产数据前会单独请您批准（G4）。 Still off. Alarms are review records and send no notifications; enabling official alerting or a production data connection will need your separate approval (G4).
